Practice Area

Conveyancing & Real Estate

Property transactions, land dealings, financing documentation and completion matters.

CLP handles property transactions, land dealings, financing documentation and completion matters for individual purchasers, vendors, developers, corporate clients and financial institutions. The practice is completion-focused, with emphasis on title review, transaction structure, consent requirements, stamping, adjudication, financing conditions and completion timelines.

How we help

  • Developer sales and sub-sale transactions (residential, commercial, industrial)
  • Perfection of transfer and perfection of charge; discharge of charge, redemption and refinancing
  • Leases, tenancies and other property-related instruments
  • Transmission and transfer of estate properties: probate, letters of administration, beneficiaries' entitlement, assent, transfers by personal representatives
  • Banking and financing documentation: loan agreements, charges, guarantees, security documents, bridging and end-financing

Representative experience

Sub-sale transactions across residential, commercial and industrial properties; developer sale and purchase; perfection matters; redemption and refinancing; estate property transfers involving beneficiaries and family arrangements; commercial tenancies; loan and security documentation connected with property financing.
